## ALL Applied AI Network

<a href="https://all-ai-network.org"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/all--ai--network.org-visit-6E40C9?style=for-the-badge" /></a>
<a href="https://github.com/ALL-Applied-AI-Network"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-ALL--Applied--AI--Network-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github" /></a>

Student-run AI chapters where students build real projects for real companies and get hired from the room. Chapters pay nothing, and we take no cut. The network has 850+ members across 11 chapters. The founding chapter, the MSOE AI Club, has close to 700, with speakers from NVIDIA, Direct Supply, Kohl's and Generac.

I lead the expansion to new universities and contribute to the platform behind it: the sponsor portal, the chapter dashboard, and the open-source [chapter-site template](https://github.com/ALL-Applied-AI-Network/aain-hub-template). The platform is running the Rockwell Automation × NVIDIA hackathon right now, with a repo for every team.

## Projects

| Project | What it is | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| **[Revit-to-Robot Digital Twin](https://github.com/manchado53/Isaac-Sim-MSOE)** | Turns an architect's Revit model into an NVIDIA Isaac Sim twin of a building that was still under construction. Synthetic labeled data, a LoRA-tuned VLM (97% on held-out door and cracked-glass frames), and a Nova Carter robot patrolling with ROS 2 Nav2. Rosie Supercomputer Challenge 2026 finalist (Honorable Mention), MICS 2026 paper. | <img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,pytorch,ros" height="24"/> |
| **[AI-Driven Soccer Analysis](https://github.com/manchado53/AI-Driven-Soccer-Analysis)** | Turns video from one cheap camera into match stats for a D3 team: player and ball tracking, speed, distance, possession and heatmaps. Custom YOLOv8 field-keypoint model. [Paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08722), published at MICS. Rosie Supercomputer Challenge 2025 finalist, 3rd place. | <img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,pytorch,opencv" height="24"/> |
| **[Consequentialism: Counterfactual Sampling to Speed Learning](https://github.com/manchado53/counterfactual-reasoning)** | Reinforcement learning research with Dr. Jeremy Kedziora, aiming for ICML 2027. At each step the agent asks "what if I had acted differently?", rolls out the other actions, and scores how much that choice changed the future. Replay then favors the moments that mattered, so it learns from fewer samples. Built in JAX, runs on MSOE's Rosie supercomputer. | <img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,linux" height="24"/> JAX |
| **[MAIC × Minoic Research](https://github.com/manchado53/maic-minoic-research)** | MSOE AI Club research group I mentor, with partner [Minoic](https://minoic.ai). A vision-language model plans a long job for a wheel loader (like clearing snow from a walkway), a rule checker blocks impossible steps, and scripted skills run it in simulation. We test whether it works on scenes and simulators it has never seen. Target: IROS 2027, early stage. | <img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py" height="24"/> MuJoCo, Drake |
| **Milwaukee Bucks Shot Similarity** | Search engine over 7,000+ 3D motion-capture shots (the dataset is now ~22,000), built for the Bucks' medical and analytics staff. Finds shots where the body moves the same way, regardless of court spot or make/miss. (Code is private.) | <img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,pytorch,fastapi,react" height="24"/> |
| **[Office Guide Robot (Direct Supply)](https://github.com/manchadito09/Robot-DS)** | Summer 2026 build with my brother [Rodrigo](https://github.com/manchadito09). A Hiwonder JetRover on a Jetson Orin Nano that guides visitors around the office: "take me to X", it leads the way and talks. ROS 2 + Nav2 on a LiDAR SLAM map, a depth camera for low obstacles, Claude as the voice brain, and a web app you open from your phone with a QR code. Rodrigo and I built it together with Claude Code. I picked the hardware, set the scope, wrote the business case, built parts of the voice, navigation and web stack, and ran the demo. | <img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,ros,linux" height="24"/> |
| **[raider-stats.com](https://raider-stats.com/)** | Stats site for the MSOE Men's Soccer team, built with [Jonny Keane](https://github.com/JKeane4210). Coaches set up practices, the app builds balanced teams, and players log game stats from their phones. 40+ users. | <img src="https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=fastapi,react,docker,aws" height="24"/> |
